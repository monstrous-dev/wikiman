# Fixture DLQ is genuinely unwired; ground truth must bless code-truthful readings

The planted DLQ trap in the e2e fixture has no producer — the S6 agent's 'contrarian' claim that it was unwired was code-accurate. The ground-truth rubric was corrected to bless code-accurate readings of planted traps rather than the fixture's original (wrong) expectation.
